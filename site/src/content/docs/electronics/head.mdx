<Steps>

1. Fit each camera to the **back** of the sensor mount with four <Hw>M2×5</Hw> self-tapping screws. Snug them gently:
   they cut their own thread in the plastic. The two cameras sit 47 mm apart and face the same way, for stereo.
2. Plug the camera cables' **Standard** ends into the cameras: the cable marked `R` into the right camera (the robot's
   right).
3. Put four M3 nuts into the front cover's pockets, set the sensor mount in, and screw it with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.
4. Put M3 nuts into the front top and bottom shoulder blocks (the pockets facing the head). Fold the spare cable length
   flat, slide the head onto the front shoulder plate, and screw it on from above and below
   with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Draft: check the lens position">
The lens window position comes from published camera figures, not an official drawing. If a lens isn't centred in its
window, the parameter is `Cam_Lens_From_Top` in `cad/Sensormount_DualCam.FCStd`.
</Aside>
