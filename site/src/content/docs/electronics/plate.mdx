## 1. IMU

Fit the GY-521 onto its posts on the plate (the posts come from Kooba's plate design), chip side up. Solder four leads
to it first (VCC, GND, SCL, SDA, about 10 cm), since it's hard to reach once the boards are in.

<Aside type="caution" title="Draft: confirm the GY-521 fixing">
Check how the GY-521 sits on its posts on the first build: a press fit, or melted over, or held with a small screw.
</Aside>

## 2. Camera cables onto the carrier

Do this before the carrier goes in: the connectors are on the carrier, and the cables run forwards **under** the
SZBK07.

<Steps>

1. Open each camera connector's latch on the carrier, push in the **Mini** end of a 200 mm cable (contacts facing the
   right way, see the carrier's marking) and close the latch.
2. The connector **nearer the carrier's USB-C edge** goes to the **right** camera. Mark that cable `R`.

</Steps>

## 3. Carrier, UNO Q and shield

<Steps>

1. Hook the carrier's edge **opposite its USB-C** under the plate's two fixed hooks.
2. Press the USB-C edge down past the two snap clips until they click. The carrier has no mounting holes; the four pads,
   two hooks and two clips hold it.
3. Press the UNO Q onto the carrier's bottom connectors. Push over the pads, not in the middle of the board.
4. Press the servo shield onto the UNO Q, servo plugs pointing forwards.

</Steps>

The UNO Q's USB-C port faces the right side rail, 20 mm away: use a right-angle USB-C plug or program it over Wi-Fi
once it's in.

## 4. SZBK07

<Steps>

1. Lay the two camera cables flat along the plate towards the front.
2. Screw the SZBK07 onto its four posts over them with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>. The screws thread into the pilot holes; no
   nuts.

</Steps>

<Render id="camera-cables" caption="Camera cable routes: under the SZBK07, through the front shoulder plates" start="exploded" />

The cables run under the SZBK07 and out through the front shoulder plates to the head. The modelled route uses about
172 mm of each 200 mm cable, leaving about 28 mm to fold flat inside the shoulder box.
