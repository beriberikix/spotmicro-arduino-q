## 1. IMU

The IMU sits on four posts in the strip between the board stack and the right side rail, chip side up, with its two
STEMMA QT sockets facing front and rear.

<Steps>

1. Plug one end of the 200 mm STEMMA QT cable into the IMU's **front** socket.
2. Screw the IMU onto its four posts with four <Hw>M2×8</Hw>. They cut their own thread in the pilot holes: snug,
   not tight.

</Steps>

The cable's other end goes into the UNO Q in step 3. Plates printed before 22 November 2026 have two pins for a
GY-521 here instead of four posts. On those, stick the Adafruit board flat in the same strip with double-sided foam
tape, edges square to the plate.

<Aside type="note">
The UNO Q's Qwiic socket is its second I2C bus (`Wire1`), separate from the shield's (`Wire`). Some early UNO Q
software releases had trouble with `Wire1`. If the IMU doesn't show up on it, cut the cable and solder its four wires
to the shield's 3V, GND, SDA and SCL instead: the IMU then shares the shield's bus (address 0x68, no clash with 0x40).
</Aside>

## 2. Camera cables onto the carrier

Do this before the carrier goes in: the connectors are on the carrier, and the cables run forwards **under** the
SZBK07.

<Steps>

1. Open each camera connector's latch on the carrier, push in the **Mini** end of a 200 mm cable (contacts facing the
   right way, see the carrier's marking) and close the latch.
2. The connector **nearer the carrier's USB-C edge** goes to the **right** camera. Mark that cable `R`.

</Steps>

## 3. Carrier, UNO Q and shield

<Steps>

1. Hook the carrier's edge **opposite its USB-C** under the plate's two fixed hooks.
2. Press the USB-C edge down past the two snap clips until they click. The carrier has no mounting holes; the four pads,
   two hooks and two clips hold it.
3. Press the UNO Q onto the carrier's bottom connectors. Push over the pads, not in the middle of the board.
4. Plug the IMU cable into the UNO Q's **Qwiic socket**, on the board's left edge, now: the shield covers it once it's
   on. Run the cable forwards along the left side, across the plate in front of the board stack, and back to the IMU,
   flat on the plate.
5. Press the servo shield onto the UNO Q, servo plugs pointing forwards. The IMU cable passes under the servo plugs.

</Steps>

The UNO Q's USB-C port faces the right side rail, 20 mm away: use a right-angle USB-C plug or program it over Wi-Fi
once it's in.

## 4. SZBK07

<Steps>

1. Lay the two camera cables flat along the plate towards the front.
2. Screw the SZBK07 onto its four posts over them with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>. The screws thread into the pilot holes; no
   nuts.

</Steps>

<Render id="camera-cables" caption="Camera cable routes: under the SZBK07, through the front shoulder plates" start="exploded" />

The cables run under the SZBK07 and out through the front shoulder plates to the head. The modelled route uses about
172 mm of each 200 mm cable, leaving about 28 mm to fold flat inside the shoulder box.
