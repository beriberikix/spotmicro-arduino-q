## Plug in and dress the leads, one leg at a time

Each leg's three leads (shoulder, hip, knee) come up into the body through the **oval window in the top shoulder
block**: the flat printed piece between the two curved shoulder plates at each end. Both legs at that end share the
window. Do one leg completely, test it, then move to the next: a mistake then only means redoing one leg.

You need about 12 small zip ties or Velcro straps.

<Check id="servo-lead-route" title="Where the leads enter the body">
The pictures below bring the leads up through the top shoulder block's oval window. From there they would have to
cross the top edge of the inner shoulder plate, and the top cover sits directly on that edge, so it would crush them.
The likely route is through the inner shoulder plate's central window instead (the one the camera cables use at the
front and the power wires at the rear), then along each side. Confirm it on the first build before relying on the
pictures.
</Check>

<ServoCableSteps />

<Steps>

1. When you plug in, use the labels from [Test and label the servos](../../prepare/servos/) and put the **brown/black
   wire on the GND row**. If a lead isn't labelled, plug it in, send `t <ch>` and check which joint moves.
2. **Left side:** the power bundle already runs down there. Lay the left legs' leads beside it or on top of it, not
   under it.
3. **Right side:** lay the right legs' leads over the IMU and its cable, not under them.
4. **Keep the top flat.** Nothing may cross over the servo plugs or loop above the board stack: the top cover clears
   the plugs by only 1.8 mm.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
To test: put the robot on its [stand](../../prepare/stand/), plug in the XT60, check the SZBK07's ON/OFF switch is at
ON, press the power button, and run servo_tool. Send `t <ch>` for each channel: each joint should move the leg you
expect. If not, two plugs are swapped.
</Aside>
