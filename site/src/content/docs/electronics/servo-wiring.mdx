<Steps>

1. Bring each leg's three leads (shoulder, hip, knee) in through its shoulder block.
2. Run them sideways into the channels beside the SZBK07: there's about **23 mm** of room on the left and **19 mm** on
   the right.
3. Plug each into its channel on the shield's right-angle headers, **brown/black wire to the GND row**. Use the labels
   from [Test and centre the servos](../../prepare/servos/).
4. Fold the extra length into the shoulder boxes, not over the board stack: the top cover clears the servo plugs by only
   1.8 mm.
5. Connect the GY-521: VCC to **3.3 V**, GND, SDA and SCL to the shield's I2C pins (it shares the bus with the PCA9685,
   at address 0x68).

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Check every joint once wired: put the robot on its stand, power the servos and the UNO Q, run servo_tool and send `t`
for each channel. Each joint should move the leg you expect. If not, two plugs are swapped.
</Aside>
