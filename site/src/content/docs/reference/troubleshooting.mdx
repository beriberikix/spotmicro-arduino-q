title: Troubleshooting
description: Common problems when building a Spot Micro, and fixes.
---

## Mechanical

| Problem | Fix |
| ------- | --- |
| Nuts fall out of their pockets while closing parts | A dab of Blu-Tack on each nut. Watch the two outer nuts by the upper-leg servo pocket in particular. |
| A bearing won't go in | Press, don't hammer: hammering cracks the part. Scrape or sand the seat lightly and press again. |
| A bearing is loose | Check the printer's X-Y hole compensation. As a last resort, a drop of CA glue on the outer race only, never the inside. |
| Leg doesn't fold flat, or there's a gap at rest | The horn is a tooth off. Refit the horn on its plate at a different arm until the plate sits square. |
| A joint can't reach its reference pose | Refit that horn one spline tooth over. |
| Upper leg wobbles on the hip | The shoulder cover's bearing isn't on its pin, or the cover screws are loose. |
| Cables won't pull through the top shell | Open the shells slightly; avoid sleeving. |
| Horn screws poke through and rub the servo | Thread an M2 nut onto each M2×8 as a spacer, or use M2×6. |
| A part snapped at a thin point | Reprint with more walls (3–4) and check the infill matches the [stl list](https://github.com/beriberikix/spotmicro-arduino-q/blob/main/stl/README.md). |

## Servos and power

| Problem | Fix |
| ------- | --- |
| A servo stutters or buzzes while holding still | It's pushing against a mechanical stop: check its horn alignment. If it does it on the bench with no load, replace it. |
| A servo turns ¾ of a turn for `s ch 0` → `s ch 180` | It's a 270° servo. Replace it with a 180° one. |
| A servo cuts out after a few seconds of pushing | DS3218 overload protection at stall. Normal standing and walking don't trigger it. |
| Servo got hot or died | Check the SZBK07 output is 6.0 V with a meter, and look for plastic blocking the joint: a stalled servo blocked by a part burns out. |
| `PCA9685 not found at 0x40` | Shield not seated, SJ1 not bridged (VCC floating), or a solder bridge on the address pads. |
| Servos twitch when another moves | Add or enlarge the capacitor on C2 (Adafruit: about 100 µF per servo); shorten the SZBK07 → V+ lead. |
| UNO Q resets when servos move | VIN dipping: check the battery charge, and that the UNO Q's VIN lead is taken before the SZBK07, not from the servo rail. |
| Nothing in the Serial Monitor | Use App Lab's **Console → Serial Monitor**; `Serial` goes through the UNO Q's Linux side, not the STM32's USB. |
