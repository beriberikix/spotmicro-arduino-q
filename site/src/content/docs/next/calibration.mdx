Planned contents:

- **Per-joint servo centres** against a reference pose: match each joint to an on-screen
  "expected pose" and nudge its pulse width until the leg matches.
- **Two reference angles per joint**: judge angles
  by the joint axes, not by the printed parts, and favour consistency over accuracy.
- **PWM per degree:** a 180° DS3218 on 500–2500 µs is about 11.1 µs per degree (2.28 PCA9685 counts at 50 Hz). Measure it
  rather than assuming a round number, or angles come out short.
- **IMU levelling** with the robot propped level on the helper stand, legs hanging: a robot resting on its folded legs
  isn't level.
