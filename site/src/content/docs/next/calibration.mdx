Planned contents, drawing on the best calibration write-ups found while researching this guide:

- **Per-joint servo centres** against a reference pose, like Leika's app: match each joint to an on-screen
  "expected pose" and nudge its pulse width until the leg matches.
- **Two reference angles per joint**, as in mike4192's
  [servo calibration guide](https://github.com/mike4192/spotMicro/blob/master/docs/servo_calibration.md): judge angles
  by the joint axes, not by the printed parts, and favour consistency over accuracy.
- **PWM per degree:** a 180° DS3218 on 500–2500 µs is about 11.1 µs per degree (2.28 PCA9685 counts at 50 Hz). Leika's
  model assumes 2.0 counts per degree, so angles would come out about 12% short unless measured.
- **IMU levelling** with the robot propped level on the helper stand, legs hanging (Leika: "a robot lying limp on its
  folded legs is not" level).
