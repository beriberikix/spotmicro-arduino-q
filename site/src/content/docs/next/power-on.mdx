Planned contents:

- Pre-flight checks: meter checks on VIN and the servo rail, no cables over the servo plugs, legs free on the stand.
- Power the UNO Q alone first (servo switch off or XT60 to the SZBK07 unplugged), confirm it boots and joins Wi-Fi.
- Check the I2C bus sees the PCA9685 (0x40) and the GY-521 (0x68).
- Bring up the servo rail with all outputs off, then enable joints one at a time.
- Current draw at idle and while standing.

Until then, [servo_tool](../../prepare/servos/) is the way to move the joints.
