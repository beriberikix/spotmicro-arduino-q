## Battery

<Render id="battery" caption="The two battery rails and the pack under the plate, seen from below" />

The pack hangs under the plate on two hook-and-loop straps that run round its width, through slots in two battery
rails, one each side of it. The rails screw up into pilot holes in the plate's underside: no nuts. The bottom cover's
floor sits 34 mm below the plate, which leaves room for the pack and the straps under it.

<Steps>

1. **Rails.** With the robot on its back, hold a rail against the plate's underside on each side of the middle, its wall
   on the inside, and screw it up into the three pilot holes with three <Hw>M3×8</Hw>. Snug, not tight: they cut their
   own thread.
2. **Straps.** Thread each strap through a slot in the left rail from the outside, fold its end back on itself and
   press it closed.
3. **Pack.** Lay the pack against the plate's underside between the rails, leads at the rear, over the straps' loose
   ends. The leads leave the pack's end at its corners: let them come out sideways.
4. **Close the straps.** Take each strap under the pack and up through the matching slot in the right rail from the
   inside. Pull it tight and fold it back on itself.
5. **Main lead.** Take the XT60 lead up through the plate's **rear-left slot**.
6. **Balance lead.** Tuck it beside the pack.

</Steps>

To charge or swap the battery later, take the bottom cover off and open the straps on the right.

## Covers

<Steps>

1. **Join the halves.** Screw the front and rear top cover halves together with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw> and nuts; same for
   the bottom cover.
2. **Nuts.** Put M3 nuts into the remaining pockets of the top and bottom shoulder blocks, front and rear.
3. **Rear cover.** Fit the switch into its 19 mm hole, slide the rear cover onto the rear shoulder plate, and screw it on
   with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.
4. **Bottom cover.** Place it under the body and screw it on with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.
5. **Top cover.** Check no cable sits on top of the servo plugs (1.8 mm clearance), then screw the top cover on with four
   <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.

</Steps>

The top and bottom covers meet the head and the rear cover over the middle of the shoulder blocks. Because the blocks
are 2 mm longer than Kooba's, those seams show a gap of about 2 mm.
