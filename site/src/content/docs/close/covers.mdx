## Battery

<Steps>

1. Fit the three LiPo brackets under the plate.
2. Slide the battery into the brackets, its lead towards the **rear-left slot**.

</Steps>

## Covers

<Steps>

1. **Join the halves.** Screw the front and rear top cover halves together with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw> and nuts; same for
   the bottom cover.
2. **Nuts.** Put M3 nuts into the remaining pockets of the top and bottom shoulder blocks, front and rear.
3. **Rear cover.** Fit the switch into its 19 mm hole, slide the rear cover onto the rear shoulder plate, and screw it on
   with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.
4. **Bottom cover.** Place it under the body and screw it on with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.
5. **Top cover.** Check no cable sits on top of the servo plugs (1.8 mm clearance), then screw the top cover on with four
   <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.

</Steps>
