## The build at a glance

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="1 · Prepare" icon="setting">
		Check the fit-test prints, clean up the parts, solder the servo shield, set the regulator to 6.0 V, then test and pre-position all twelve servos.
	</Card>
	<Card title="2–3 · Legs and shoulders" icon="puzzle">
		Build four legs from the foot up: lower leg, knee, upper leg, hip and shoulder joint, then add the reinforced shoulder cover.
	</Card>
	<Card title="4 · Body" icon="seti:config">
		Build the frame around the electronics plate and fit the front and rear leg pairs.
	</Card>
	<Card title="5–6 · Electronics and covers" icon="laptop">
		Fit the board stack, power wiring, servo leads, camera head and covers.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
