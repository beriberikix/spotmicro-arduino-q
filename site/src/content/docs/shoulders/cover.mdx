Without it, the upper leg hangs on the hip servo's shaft alone, which wobbles. The shoulder cover bridges over the top of the hip and holds a second bearing on the outside of the upper leg, so the leg is
supported on both sides.

<Parts per="per leg" total={4} bag="D" items={[
	[1, 'Shoulder cover', 'Symmetric: the same part on every leg'],
	[1, '625ZZ bearing'],
	['2+', '<kbd class="hw">M3×20</kbd> + M3 nuts', 'One per hole that lines up with the cap'],
]} tools={['2.5 mm hex', 'Vice or flat block']} />

<Render id="shoulder-cover" caption="Shoulder cover over the hip, onto the cap extension and the bearing pin" />

<Steps>

1. Press a 625ZZ into the cover's 16.3 mm bore, evenly, without a hammer.
2. Slide the cover's bearing over the M3 bearing pin on the upper leg's top shell, and bring its other end against the
   extended servo cap.
3. Bolt the cover to the cap with <Hw>M3×20</Hw> and M3 nuts. The screw heads and nuts sit in recesses.
4. Move the leg through its range by hand (servo unpowered): it should swing freely, now without play.

</Steps>
