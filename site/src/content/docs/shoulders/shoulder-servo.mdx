<Steps>

1. Plug the shoulder servo into the rig and send `a <ch>` (channels 0, 3, 6, 9): **90°** for all four.
2. Fit its four rubber dampeners.
3. Put four M3 nuts into the servo pockets of the mount.
4. Drop the servo into its pocket, output shaft facing **out of the end of the robot** (towards the nose for front legs,
   the tail for rear legs), and screw it in with four <Hw>M3×20</Hw>.
5. `o <ch>`, unplug.

</Steps>

The shoulder servo's horn isn't fitted here; it sits on the outer shoulder plate and goes on in the
[body section](../../body/install-shoulders/).
