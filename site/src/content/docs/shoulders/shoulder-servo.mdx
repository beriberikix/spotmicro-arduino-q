The parts are the last ones in bag D, left over from the hip joint. Each leg takes its own shoulder servo, tagged `xx-S`:

<LegPick joint="shoulder" />

<Render id="shoulder-servo" caption="Shoulder servo into the shoulder servo mount" />

<Steps>

1. Fit the servo's four rubber dampeners.
2. Put four M3 nuts into the servo pockets of the mount.
3. Drop the servo into the mount from its open side, with the **output shaft poking out of the side wall opposite the
   bearing pin** and the **lead coming out through the cable slot** at the top.
4. Screw it in with four <Hw>M3×20</Hw>.

</Steps>

No angle to set here: nothing is fixed to the shaft yet. The shoulder servo's horn sits on the outer shoulder plate
and goes on, at 90°, in the [body section](../../body/install-shoulders/).
