The **shoulder joint** is robjk's extended servo mount plus the extended servo cap. The cap carries the horn that the
hip servo drives, and its extension is where the shoulder cover will bolt on.

<Aside type="caution" title="Which servo mount?">
Both shoulder servos on a side face **outwards**: the front one towards the nose, the rear one towards the tail. So each
side uses **one normal and one mirrored** mount (Kubina: "the shoulder joint uses the mirrored parts for the front or
rear leg on both sides"). Pair them up before you start: FL with RR are the same part, and FR with RL. Hold a mount
against its leg and check that the servo pocket opens towards the correct end of the robot.
</Aside>

## 1. Horn onto the extended cap

<Render id="hip-horn-cap" caption="Horn onto the extended servo cap" />

Same as the knee: M2×8 screws with nuts threaded on as spacers, through the middle hole of each arm.

## 2. Bearing pin onto the servo mount

<Render id="shoulder-pin" caption="Bearing pin on the back of the shoulder servo mount" />

<Steps>

1. Put one M2 nut in the small pocket in the servo mount.
2. Screw an <Hw>M2×8</Hw> through the bearing pin into it. This pin will turn in the bearing in the inner shoulder plate.

</Steps>

## 3. Cap onto the servo mount

<Render id="cap-to-mount" caption="Extended cap with horn onto the shoulder servo mount" />

<Steps>

1. Put four M3 nuts into the pockets in the base of the servo mount.
2. Screw the cap on with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.

</Steps>

## 4. Shoulder joint onto the hip servo

<Render id="shoulder-to-leg" caption="Shoulder joint onto the upper leg's hip servo" />

<Steps>

1. Plug the hip servo into the rig and send its assembly angle: **120° for left legs, 60° for right legs** (channels
   1, 4, 7, 10). Leave it powered.
2. Kubina's alignment rule: don't line the shoulder joint up with the horn's **top** arm, but with **the next arm to the
   right on left legs, or to the left on right legs**. Each arm is 60°, and this puts the upper leg at its standing angle.
   The upper leg then deliberately swings further backwards than forwards.
3. Push the cap's horn onto the hip servo shaft and screw it on with an <Hw>M3×8</Hw> through the horn centre.
4. Send `o <ch>` and unplug.

</Steps>
