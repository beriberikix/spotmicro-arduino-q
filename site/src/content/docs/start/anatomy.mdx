## Directions

- **Front** is the head end. **Left** and **right** are the robot's own left and right, as if you were standing behind
  it.
- **Inner** means towards the robot's centre line; **outer** means away from it.
- Legs are named **FL**, **FR**, **RL**, **RR** (front-left, front-right, rear-left, rear-right).

## Joints

Each leg has three servos:

| Joint | Moves | Servo sits in | Horn is fixed to |
| ----- | ----- | ------------- | ---------------- |
| **Shoulder** | the whole leg sideways (in and out) | the shoulder servo mount | the outer shoulder plate (chassis) |
| **Hip** | the upper leg forwards and back | the upper-leg shells | the extended servo cap on the shoulder joint |
| **Knee** | the lower leg | the lower leg (wrist and wrist counterpart) | the servohorn mount plate on the upper leg |

Every joint has a 625ZZ bearing opposite the servo, so the part is held on both sides: the knee on the ball-bearing
mount plate, the hip in robjk's shoulder cover, and the shoulder in the inner shoulder plate.

| wrist | `legs/Wrist.stl` (the lower leg's main part) |

## Left and right parts

Leg parts come in pairs: the normal STL is for the **left** legs and `_mirrored` is for the **right** legs. The
shoulder servo mount is the exception, as Kubina points out: each side gets one normal and one mirrored mount, one for
the front leg and one for the rear, so that both shoulder servo outputs face outwards (towards the nose at the front
and the tail at the back).

Mark every part with a pencil or a dot of paint marker as soon as it comes off the printer: **FL**, **FR**, **RL**,
**RR**. Mixed-up mirrored parts are the most common way to lose an afternoon.
