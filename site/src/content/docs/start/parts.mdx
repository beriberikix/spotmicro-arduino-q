	'12× 625ZZ bearings, unflanged',
	'A short strip of Kapton or electrical tape (under the SZBK07, over the camera cables)',
]} />

The robot uses just three screw sizes (the optional stand adds M3×12 and M3×16), so buy bulk packs: a few spare M3 nuts will save you when one rolls under
the bench.
