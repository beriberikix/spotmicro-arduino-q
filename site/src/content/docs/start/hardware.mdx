Sorting the hardware first turns a 250-screw build into a series of small, countable kits, and you'll see straight away
if anything is missing. Use small zip bags, cups or an egg carton, and label each one with its letter.

Each bag's first table lists its hardware step by step; the **total** under it is what to count into the bag,
with a box to tick as you pack it. Bags A–D are **per leg**: make four of each, so their totals give both one bag and
all four. [Totals](#totals) at the end adds up every bag. Bag H is for the optional helper stand.

The bags also take one printed part: the **bearing pins**. A bearing pin is a small peg, 5 mm across, that a 625ZZ
bearing fits onto, held down by a screw through its centre. There are two kinds, told apart by the hole: eight with an
M2 hole (`Shoulder_Joint_Ball_Bearing_Pin`) and four with a larger M3 hole (`Shoulder_Joint_Ball_Bearing_Pin_M3x8`).
Take them from your printed parts as you fill the bags.

<Aside type="tip" title="Pre-thread the horn screws">
For bags B, D and E, screw an M2 nut all the way onto each M2×8 now. The nut is a spacer: a bare M2×8
pokes through the horn and rubs on the servo. If you have M2×6 screws, use those instead and skip the nuts. Two screws per
horn are enough, if you're short.
</Aside>
