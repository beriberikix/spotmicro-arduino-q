Sorting the hardware first turns a 250-screw build into a series of small, countable kits, and you'll see straight away
if anything is missing. Use small zip bags, cups or an egg carton, and label each one with its letter.

The counts below come from Kubina's assembly guide and the project's CAD model, step by step. Bags A–D are **per leg**:
make four of each.

<Aside type="tip" title="Pre-thread the horn screws">
For bags B, D and E, screw an M2 nut all the way onto each M2×8 now. Kubina uses the nut as a spacer: a bare M2×8
pokes through the horn and rubs on the servo. If you have M2×6 screws, use those instead and skip the nuts. Kubina also
notes that two screws per horn are enough, if you're short.
</Aside>
