This guide walks you through building **SpotMicro for Arduino UNO Q**, a 3D-printed robot dog with twelve servos, an
[Arduino UNO Q](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/uno-q/) brain, an
[UNO Media Carrier](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/uno-media-carrier/) and two Raspberry Pi Camera Module 2s in the
head. Every printable part is in the [repository](https://github.com/beriberikix/spotmicro-arduino-q/tree/main/stl).

## How to use it

- **Read each section once before you start it.** Several steps depend on the servo being at the right angle *before*
  a horn goes on. Kubina's original guide says the same, in bold.
- **Build the front-left leg first, end to end, then the other three.** The first leg teaches you the parts; the next
  three go much faster. Pages show the front-left leg; the others are the same or mirrored, and the page says which.
- **Check things off.** The ✓ boxes save your progress in this browser, and the [home page](../../) adds them up.
- **Watch the animation.** Each step has a CAD render with three views: an animation of the parts going together, an
  exploded view with the new parts in <span class="new-part">orange</span>, and the finished step.

<Aside type="caution" title="This is a first draft">
The renders come from the project's FreeCAD model, not from a real build yet. Steps marked with a purple **Photo to take**
or **Video to shoot** box will get real photos and clips. If something here doesn't match your parts, trust the parts
and [open an issue](https://github.com/beriberikix/spotmicro-arduino-q/issues).
</Aside>

## What it builds on

The frame is Michael Kubina's [SpotMicroESP32](https://github.com/michaelkubina/SpotMicroESP32), published on
Thingiverse as "Kooba", a support-free remix of Deok-yeon Kim's original
[Spot Micro](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3445283). The shoulders are robjk's
[reinforced shoulder remix](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4937631). The electronics plate and camera head are this
project's own. The firmware will be a port of Rune Harlyk's
[Leika](https://github.com/runeharlyk/SpotMicroESP32-Leika).

The leg, shoulder and body steps follow the order of Kubina's
[assembly guide](https://github.com/michaelkubina/SpotMicroESP32/tree/master/assembly). This guide adds robjk's
shoulder covers, the UNO Q electronics, per-step hardware counts, a servo tool sketch and a single table of servo angles.
See [Credits and sources](../../reference/credits/) for everything this guide learned from.

## Time

| Stage | Time |
| ----- | ---- |
| Printing (full robot, P1S) | about 32 h |
| Fit check, cleanup, sorting hardware | 2–3 h |
| Shield soldering, servo testing | 2 h |
| Four legs with shoulders | 4–6 h (the first leg is the slowest) |
| Body, electronics, covers | 3–4 h |
