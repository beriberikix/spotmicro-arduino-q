The two knee plates straddle the lower leg: the **servohorn mount** grips the knee servo's shaft on the inner side, and
the **ball-bearing mount** rides on the bearing pin on the outer side. The upper leg is bolted between them on the next
page.

<Aside type="note" title="The renders show the standing pose">
The CAD model stands the robot up, so the renders show the knee bent. You assemble it **folded flat**, as described
below.
</Aside>

## 1. Horn onto the servohorn mount

<Render id="knee-horn" caption="Six-arm horn onto the servohorn mount" />

<Steps>

1. Thread an M2 nut all the way onto each <Hw>M2×8</Hw> (if you didn't when sorting). The nut is a spacer: a bare M2×8
   would poke through and rub on the servo.
2. Lay the horn in the recess on the mount's inner face, splined boss facing out.
3. Screw it on through the **middle hole of each arm** into the mount.

</Steps>

## 2. Mount onto the knee servo

<Render id="knee-horn-plate" caption="Servohorn mount onto the knee servo's shaft" />

<Steps>

1. Plug the knee servo into its channel on the bench rig and send `a <ch>`: **180° for left legs, 0° for right legs**
   (channels 2, 5, 8, 11; see the [angle table](../../prepare/servos/#assembly-angles)). Leave it powered.
2. Hold the servohorn mount against the knee, with the long part of the mount lying **folded flat along the lower leg**,
   as in Kubina's rest position.
3. Push the horn onto the shaft. Kubina's check: the plate's arms should be **perfectly perpendicular** to the wrist.
   The horn has 25 teeth, so it isn't symmetrical: if the plate sits a little off, pull it off, unscrew the horn from
   the mount, turn the horn to a different arm, and try again until it lines up.
4. Screw the mount to the shaft with an <Hw>M3×8</Hw> through the centre of the horn.
5. Send `o <ch>` and unplug the servo.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Getting this right matters. If the plate is a tooth off, the leg either won't fold flat at rest, or the servo will
push against the stop at 0°/180° and stutter, drawing current all the time (Kubina). If the servo can't reach a
reference pose later, refit the horn one tooth over (Leika's assembly notes).
</Aside>

## 3. Ball-bearing mount

<Render id="knee-bearing-plate" caption="Bearing into the ball-bearing mount, then onto the pin" />

<Steps>

1. Press a 625ZZ bearing into the ball-bearing mount. It's a **really tight** fit: press it in evenly with a vice or a
   flat block, never with a hammer.
2. Push the mount onto the bearing pin on the outer side of the lower leg, lined up with the servohorn mount on the
   other side.

</Steps>
