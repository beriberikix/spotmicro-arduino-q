The lower leg (the **wrist**) holds the knee servo. Build all four the same way, using normal parts for
the left legs and `_mirrored` parts for the right legs.

<Parts per="per leg" total={4} bag="A" items={[
	[1, 'Wrist', 'Normal for FL/RL, mirrored for FR/RR'],
	[1, 'Wrist counterpart', 'Same side as the wrist'],
	[1, 'Foot tip (TPU)'],
	[1, 'Bearing pin, M2 bore'],
	[1, 'DS3218 labelled <code>xx-K</code> (knee), with 4 rubber dampeners'],
	[1, 'Servo extension cable, 10–15 cm'],
	[2, '<kbd class="hw">M3×8</kbd> + 2 M3 nuts', 'Foot tip'],
	[1, '<kbd class="hw">M2×8</kbd> + 1 M2 nut', 'Bearing pin'],
	[4, '<kbd class="hw">M3×20</kbd> + 4 M3 nuts', 'Servo sandwich'],
]} tools={['2.5 mm hex', '1.5 mm hex', 'Tweezers']} />

## 1. Foot tip

<Render id="leg-foot" caption="Foot tip onto the wrist" />

<Steps>

1. Push two M3 nuts into the pockets at the bottom of the wrist.
2. Hold the TPU foot tip in place and screw it on with two <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.

</Steps>

Don't over-tighten: TPU squashes, and the screws only need to hold it snug.

## 2. Knee bearing pin

<Render id="leg-knee-pin" caption="Bearing pin onto the wrist counterpart" />

<Steps>

1. Push one M2 nut into the small pocket in the wrist counterpart.
2. Put an <Hw>M2×8</Hw> through the bearing pin (the M2-bore one) and screw it into the nut, so the pin sticks out
   of the counterpart's outer face.

</Steps>

## 3. Knee servo sandwich

<Render id="leg-knee-servo" caption="Knee servo between the wrist and its counterpart" />

<Steps>

1. Fit the four rubber dampeners to the servo's mounting ears. Fit all four, or the M3×20 won't line up.
2. Plug the **extension cable** onto the servo lead now, and put a strip of tape around the joint so it can't pull
   apart inside the leg.
3. Push four M3 nuts into the small pockets in the wrist counterpart.
4. Slide the servo into its pocket in the counterpart from above. The output shaft and the bearing pin end up on
   **opposite sides** of the knee: shaft on the inner side (towards the robot's centre line), pin on the outer side.
   Compare with the render.
5. Bring the wrist (with its foot) up against the other side and push the halves together until there are no gaps.
   Keep a finger over the nuts so none fall out.
6. Screw them together with four <Hw>M3×20</Hw>.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Nuts falling out of their pockets is the classic Spot Micro annoyance. A tiny dab of Blu-Tack on each nut holds it
while you close the parts.
</Aside>
