The upper leg is two shells around the **hip servo**. robjk's top shell has an extra hole for the bearing pin that the
shoulder cover will ride on. Both servo leads, the knee's and the hip's, leave through the hole in the top shell.

## 1. Hip servo into the bottom shell

<Render id="upper-servo" caption="Hip servo and nuts into the bottom shell" />

<Steps>

1. Fit the four rubber dampeners to the hip servo.
2. Put four M3 nuts in the holes by the servo pocket.
3. Push the servo into its pocket. The two inner nuts are now held by the servo; the two outer ones can still fall out,
   so be careful (Kubina).

</Steps>

## 2. Shoulder-cover pin on the top shell

robjk's top shell has a round hole on its outer face. The M3-bore bearing pin goes on the outside of it, and the
shoulder cover's bearing rides on that pin.

<Steps>

1. Put an <Hw>M3×8</Hw> through the hole from the inside of the top shell and screw it into the pin on the outside,
   with an M3 nut if your pin's bore doesn't grip the thread.
2. Tighten it so the pin sits square and doesn't wobble.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Draft: confirm the pin fixing">
robjk doesn't publish step-by-step instructions, and the pin isn't in the CAD assembly. Confirm which side the screw
head and nut go on during the first build.
</Aside>

## 3. Route the cables and close the shells

<Render id="upper-shells" caption="Top shell onto the bottom shell, four M3×20 through the servo" />

This is the fiddly part: there is very little room between the shells.

<Steps>

1. Feed the **knee servo's extension cable** (from the lower leg) through the **front hole** of the top shell.
2. Feed the **hip servo's lead** through the same hole.
3. Bring the shells together while gently pulling both cables through the hole. The knee cable goes into the shells
   through the gap between them, and runs in the **groove under the top shell**, which is made for it.
4. With the shells closed, check the cables still slide back and forth through the hole.
5. Screw the servo and both shells together with four <Hw>M3×20</Hw>.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Kubina warns against thick braided sleeving here: there isn't room. Bare servo cable works; if you must sleeve it, use
the thinnest you can find.
</Aside>

## 4. Bolt the shells between the knee plates

<Render id="upper-to-knee" caption="Upper leg between the servohorn mount and the ball-bearing mount" />

<Steps>

1. Put four M3 nuts into the four rear-facing pockets of the shells.
2. Slide the knee end of the shells between the two knee plates, so the holes in the plates line up with the holes in
   the shells.
3. Screw everything together with four <Hw>M3×20</Hw>.

</Steps>

The leg should now fold flat: that's the robot's resting position (Kubina). Turn the knee gently by hand, servo
unpowered: it should move without binding against the shells.
