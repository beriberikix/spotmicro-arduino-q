The frame is two **shoulder blocks** (front and rear) joined by two **chassis sides**, with the electronics plate held
between the sides.

## Inner shoulders

<Steps>

1. Put two M3 nuts into each top and each bottom shoulder block. Orient the blocks so the nut pockets that will later
   hold the **covers face into the body** (Kubina).
2. Screw each inner shoulder to a top and a bottom block with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>, so the screw heads sink into their
   pockets. The **bigger blocks go at the top**.

</Steps>

## Chassis

<Steps>

1. Put four M3 nuts into the pockets of each chassis side. The **flat side faces outwards**, and the long cut-out for
   the mounting plate is at the **bottom**.
2. Hold the electronics plate between the two sides, its edges in the cut-outs.
3. Screw both inner shoulders onto the ends of the sides with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw> each, the bigger blocks at the top.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Before the frame closes, check that the plate's rear-left slot (for the battery lead) is at the **rear left**, and that
the SZBK07 posts are towards the front. The board stack sits at the rear of the plate.
</Aside>
