The frame is two **shoulder blocks** (front and rear) joined by two **chassis sides**, with the electronics plate held
between the sides.

If you printed the MakerWorld profile, the parts are on these
[plates](../../prepare/prints/#check-you-have-every-part), named as in Bambu Studio:

- **Inner shoulders:** Plate 1, *Chassis plates and rails*. The plate also has the two **outer** shoulders, which go on
  later: the inner ones have round bearing seats for the shoulder pins, the outer ones star-shaped recesses for servo
  horns.
- **Chassis sides:** Plate 1, *Chassis plates and rails*.
- **Top and bottom shoulder blocks:** Plate 2, *Electronics plate and chassis blocks*. They're **49.5 mm** long.
- **UNO Q mounting plate:** Plate 2, *Electronics plate and chassis blocks*. Leave the three LiPo brackets on that plate
  for later.

<Aside type="caution" title="Shoulder blocks printed before 22 November 2026">
The blocks are 2 mm longer than Kooba's originals, so the legs have room between the inner and outer shoulders.
Measure yours: if they're **47.5 mm**, reprint all four. With the short blocks the outer shoulder stands about 2 mm
off the block ends and can't be screwed down.
</Aside>

## Inner shoulders

Build both inner shoulders the same way, one for the front and one for the rear.

<Render id="inner-shoulder" caption="Top and bottom shoulder blocks onto an inner shoulder" />

<Steps>

1. Drop two M3 nuts into each block: into the two **rectangular slots on its top face, at the end that touches the
   inner shoulder**. They stand upright in the slots.
2. Hold the blocks against the flat face of the inner shoulder, the **bigger block at the top** and the smaller one at
   the bottom, their nut ends against the plate.
3. Screw each block on with two <Hw>M3×8</Hw> from the other side of the inner shoulder, through the plate and into
   the nuts.

</Steps>

## Chassis

<Render id="frame" caption="Inner shoulders, chassis sides and electronics plate" />

<Steps>

1. Put four M3 nuts into the pockets of each chassis side. The **flat side faces outwards**, and the long cut-out for
   the mounting plate is at the **bottom**.
2. Hold the electronics plate between the two sides, its edges in the cut-outs.
3. Screw both inner shoulders onto the ends of the sides with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw> each, the bigger blocks at the top.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Before the frame closes, check that the plate's rear-left slot (for the battery lead) is at the **rear left**, and that
the SZBK07 posts are towards the front. The board stack sits at the rear of the plate.
</Aside>
