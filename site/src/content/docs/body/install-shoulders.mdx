Each pair of legs (front, rear) hangs between an **outer shoulder plate**, which holds the two shoulder servo horns,
and the **inner shoulder** in the frame, which holds the bearings for the shoulder pins.

<Parts bag="E" items={[
	[2, 'Outer shoulders'],
	[4, '25T six-arm servo horns'],
	[24, '<kbd class="hw">M2×8</kbd> with M2 nuts threaded on', 'Horns to the outer shoulders'],
	[4, '<kbd class="hw">M3×8</kbd>', 'Outer shoulders to the shoulder servo shafts'],
	[4, '625ZZ bearings', 'On the shoulder pins'],
	[8, '<kbd class="hw">M3×8</kbd> + 8 M3 nuts', 'Outer shoulders to the blocks'],

## 1. Horns onto the outer shoulders

<Render id="outer-shoulder-horns" caption="Two horns onto an outer shoulder plate" />

Screw two horns to each outer shoulder plate with 12 M2×8 (with nuts) per plate, through the middle hole of each arm.

## 2. Leg pairs onto the outer shoulders

<Render id="legs-to-outer" caption="Front legs onto the front outer shoulder" />

<Steps>

1. Plug both front shoulder servos into the rig (channels 0 and 3) and send `a 0` and `a 3`: **90°**.
2. Hold the two front legs hanging **straight down**, and push the outer shoulder's horns onto both shoulder servo
   shafts. The horn arms should be **perfectly perpendicular** (Kubina); if not, unscrew a horn, turn it to another arm
   and refit it.
3. Screw each shaft to its horn with an <Hw>M3×8</Hw> through the horn centre.
4. `o`, unplug, and repeat for the rear legs (channels 6 and 9).

</Steps>

## 3. Pairs onto the frame

<Render id="install-shoulders" caption="Front and rear leg pairs onto the frame" />

<Steps>

1. Press a 625ZZ bearing onto each shoulder bearing pin (at the inner end of each shoulder servo mount).
2. Put M3 nuts into the front faces of the top and bottom shoulder blocks.
3. Slide the front leg pair onto the frame so the four bearings drop into their seats in the inner shoulder, and screw
   the outer shoulder to the blocks with four <Hw>M3×8</Hw>.
4. Repeat for the rear pair.
5. Lead all twelve servo cables up into the body, keeping each leg's three together. They plug in later.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Stand the robot on the [helper stand](https://github.com/beriberikix/spotmicro-arduino-q/tree/main/stl/optional/helper-stand)
from here on, so the legs hang free. A robot resting on folded legs isn't level, and the IMU levelling and servo
calibration both need it level (Leika).
</Aside>
