Before you build, check every place where a printed part has to fit something you bought. If you printed the
[fit-test project](https://github.com/beriberikix/spotmicro-arduino-q/blob/main/docs/test-prints.md) (about 3 hours),
use those parts. If you printed the full robot straight away, do the same checks with the real parts. Either way,
nothing gets screwed together for good yet.

## What you need

- UNO Media Carrier, UNO Q, SZBK07
- Two Camera Module 2s
- One DS3218 with its dampeners and a 25T six-arm horn
- Two 625ZZ bearings
- 4× M3×8, 8× M2×5 self-tapping, a few M3×20 + nuts, a few M2×8 + nuts

<Aside type="caution" title="Measured SZBK07 is smaller than modelled">
The SZBK07 listing linked in the BOM measures 66 × 48 × 21 mm; the plate was modelled for 60 × 54 × 28 mm. Check its
four mounting holes against the posts before anything else. If they don't line up, the post spacing is set by
`Buck_SX` / `Buck_SY` in the plate's Params spreadsheet.
</Aside>

## If something doesn't fit

The [fit-test page](https://github.com/beriberikix/spotmicro-arduino-q/blob/main/docs/test-prints.md#if-something-doesnt-fit)
lists the parameter for each problem. In short:

- **This project's parts** (plate, camera mount): change the `Params` spreadsheet and re-export.
- **Frame and shoulder parts** (servo, horn, bearing, pin fits): these have been printed by many builders, so check
  the printer first: flow calibration, then X-Y hole compensation (about +0.1 mm) for tight holes and bores.
