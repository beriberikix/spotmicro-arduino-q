title: Clean up the prints
description: Deburr, test nut pockets and bearing seats, and label every printed part.
---

Ten minutes per plate here saves an hour of fighting parts later.

1. **Remove brims and elephant's foot.** Run a deburring tool or knife along the first-layer edge of every part,
   especially faces that meet another part (the shell halves, the wrist and its counterpart, the knee plates).
2. **Clear the holes.** Push an M3 screw through every M3 hole and an M2 through every M2 hole. A screw should slide
   or turn in easily; if it binds, twist it through or chase the hole with a tap. Don't force a drill bit through.
3. **Test the nut pockets.** Drop an M3 nut into each pocket. It should seat flat and stay put when you tip the part
   over gently. If a pocket is tight, warm the nut with a soldering iron for a second and press it in. Loose ones get a
   tiny dab of Blu-Tack during assembly.
4. **Bearing seats.** Bearings are a really tight press fit. Press them in with a vice or with
   your thumb and a flat block. **Never hammer them**: that cracks the part. If a seat is
   too tight, scrape or sand the bore slightly and try again.
5. **Label everything.** Mark each leg part **FL**, **FR**, **RL** or **RR** in pencil on a hidden face. Normal STLs are
   for the left legs, `_mirrored` for the right.

<Aside type="tip">
Lay each leg's parts out in its own tray, with its hardware bags from [Sort the hardware](../../start/hardware/). The
build then becomes: take a tray, empty it.
</Aside>
